UIT ERP – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

UIT ERP is a management app used by schools and coaching institutes ("the Institute"). The Institute decides what information is entered in the app.

Information handled

Student and staff names, contact details, photos, attendance, fee payments, exam answers and scores, as entered by the Institute or by the student through the Student Portal.

How it is used

Only to run the app: showing a student their own profile, fees, attendance, notices and exams, and giving the Institute its reports. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

Storage and security

Data is stored on the device and, when the Institute turns on cloud sync, on Google Firebase servers. Data is sent over encrypted connections. Student Portal logins are protected by a password and each student's phone receives only that student's own record.

Permissions

Camera (to take photos), Notifications (reminders and notices), Internet (sync and portal).

Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties, except Google Firebase which hosts the data on the Institute's behalf.

Deleting data

Students may ask the Institute to correct or delete their data. The Institute can delete records inside the app. For any request, write to: ssangram83@gmail.com

Children

The app is used by institutes; student records for minors are entered and controlled by the Institute and parents/guardians.
